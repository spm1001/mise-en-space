## For Claudes to come

**When a filter needs a semantic judgement a deterministic system cannot make, don't approximate the semantics — change the contract so a capable consumer makes the call.** The crop filter needed "is this a chart?" and two plausible proxies each failed the same way during calibration: excluding JPEGs (photos are usually JPEG) evicted a real chart that shipped as JPEG, and a count cap (photo decks are noisy) evicted the same chart again by ranking it behind forty photos. Each proxy was measured against the census's 20 adjudicated vision-only probes before shipping, which is the only reason neither survived. What shipped instead is pure geometry — size, page-spread, page-coverage — which keeps some logos and photos, plus a contract line telling the vision-capable consumer that the semantic call is theirs. The false positives cost kilobytes; a false negative is a silently lost value. Same shape as the repo's never-narrow-a-fetch rule, arrived at from the opposite direction.

**The extraction-lane differ is now a reusable instrument.** Ghostscript's `-dFILTERIMAGE -dFILTERVECTOR` renders "what pdftotext can see" as an image; pdfminer's LTImage bboxes place the embedded objects; composite the two and you have a visual diff of any page's text lane versus its design layer (`~/notes/practices/mise/showpiece-triptychs-2026-08-18/build.py`). It found a false caption of mine within an hour of existing (the live verdict list contradicted what I'd written), it made the resident's waterfall complaint visible as floating numbers with no bars, and it exposed a rendering subtlety worth remembering: on dark decks, white glyphs render white-on-white once the background image is stripped, so the glyph panel under-shows survival — pdftotext itself reads them fine. The best design-communication device in it: when a panel would be identical to its neighbour ("nothing to restore"), don't ship the identical panel — ship what the system actually does instead (the fallback), stamped. An unchanged panel reads as a bug, never as a boundary.
