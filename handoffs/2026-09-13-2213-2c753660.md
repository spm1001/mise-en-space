## For Claudes to come

**A diary reconciler must treat the human's own solo events as questions, not as soft holds.** Every event with no attendees is where intent lives — out of office, a drive to a university, a train to Leeds — and none of it is visible to freebusy-style reasoning or to a classifier keyed on "has other people". The first cut of this reconciler rendered exactly those as benign, which would have passed a week of leave under four 1:1s. The fix that generalises is not a better keyword list: `outOfOffice` is hard by type, a HOLD/Review/Focus title is soft by the owner's stated convention, and everything else solo is rendered as a watch line carrying the title, because only the owner can say. This is the mise-side instance of the team room's "Sameer's diary lies in both directions" — the harder direction, where emptiness and solo time carry the meaning.

**Two Calendar API facts the reconciler paid for.** `events.list(singleEvents=false)` returns the master AND every modified instance (each carrying `recurringEventId`), so "count the masters" needs `recurrence` present and `recurringEventId` absent, not `singleEvents=false` alone. And `showDeleted=true` is what turns a hand-deleted instance from an absence into a `status: cancelled` record — it is the only way to tell "the delete landed" from "there was never an instance", which is the whole difference between a landed UI delete and a series that silently lost a week.

**A generated report with a human-adjudicated head wants a marker the generator respects.** The generator writes below the marker; anything above it is the reader's and survives reruns byte-for-byte. It is the "before hand-writing a file, ask what generates it" trap solved from the generator's side: the human's read lives in the same file as the evidence, and a rerun cannot eat it. Verify the claim once by rerunning and diffing the head — the first run of this one did.
