## For Claudes to come

**A coverage cue must count the population, not the survivors.** `cues.calendars_read` said "6 calendars from your calendar list" when the list held eight and two had 404'd — true of what was read, false about the list, and an event on the two that failed was absent with no other sign in the one cue a reader actually reads. The misses were in a separate warnings cue, which is the trap's second half: disclosure in a channel nobody scans is not disclosure. The same shape lurks in any "N of your X" line computed after a filter — files a search could open, threads a sweep could parse, calendars a fan-out could read. Render the denominator from the population, name the misses in the headline, and carry them as records beside the prose so a tool can count what a human can read. The adapter reports on both channels (`.warnings` for library callers, `.calendars_failed` for the tools layer); the tools layer turns the record into the headline. Same split as freebusy's 403.

**On this estate a push to `main` is a release, on the assembler's clock.** Since bds-hajeli (2026-09-09) the assembler treats unbumped drift as a reason to bump the suite, not to quarantine the plugin: gudeci's fix was live in both marketplaces as 1.85.15 with a generated CHANGELOG line before anyone thought to publish — because the daily scheduled run (nominal 07:00Z, observed ~12:20Z lately) happened to fire nine minutes after the push. It is not push-triggered, so "the next assemble" can be up to a day away. Three consequences. "NOT DONE: publish" in a close note is usually false by the next day, so say "shipped by the next assemble" and check the marketplace clone (`~/repos/spm1001/batterie`, `git show origin/main:plugins/mise/.claude-plugin/plugin.json`) rather than the cache on any one machine. `/batterie:publish` run after the assembler has already bumped mints an empty version — its dry-run shows the local suite clone's number, which can be behind origin, so the dry-run is not the check; publish is for "now, with a human-written line". And the per-repo CHANGELOG's "[Unreleased]" convention has lost its stamping moment: there is no human publish step for it to hook, which is why five shipped cards were still headed "[Unreleased]" here (mise-leceva; two stamped at this close).
