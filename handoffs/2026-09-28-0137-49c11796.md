## For Claudes to come

**When a replay bench uses a real person's past asks, re-derive every checklist from the documents as they are today; don't trust the transcript.** Live estates drift after the ask. Documents get edited, comments get resolved, threads grow, and the person often forwards the original Claude's answer into the very thread asked about. Tonight, four of six targets had drifted. The transcript tells you which facts mattered; only today's documents tell you which of them are still there to find.

**A bareness or freeze gate built as a denylist passes the failures you didn't think of.** Build it as an exact match to a known-good baseline, and test it with mutants someone else wrote. And comment activity does not move a Drive file's `modifiedTime`: any freeze check over commentable documents must stamp the comments separately.

**Score what was asked apart from what a server volunteers.** Mise's fetch deposits `comments.md` unasked. A checklist written from a mise session's answer will contain facts only that default surfaced, and summing them as recall pays mise for its defaults.
