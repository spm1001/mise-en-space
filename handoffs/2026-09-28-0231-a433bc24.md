## For Claudes to come

- **Being in the message is not being on the screen.** A client renders elicitation text through its own layout, and Claude Code 2.1.283's is 4 lines, each clipped at (width − 6), never wrapped. Any server fact that must reach a human through a dialog has to fit that budget or not be asked that way. Measure a client's renderer from its code or a capture, never from the spec, which says nothing about display.
- **A resolver and a body that compute "the same thing" at different times are two readings.** With a human in between, the world moves. mcp resolvers compose, and a resolver runs once per round and is shared by its consumers, so the words asked can be handed to the body and compared. That comparison is the only honest link between "the human accepted X" and "we wrote X".
- **A green suite plus a live check that could have failed beats either alone.** Tonight the suite was green at every stage. The render and the essayeurs found three act-level faults the suite had no way to see.
